How It Works
  1. Select a savings goal (or leave blank for general savings)
  2. Enter your Safaricom M-Pesa number
  3. Enter the amount you want to send
  4. Click Send M-Pesa Prompt — your phone will ring
  5. Enter your M-Pesa PIN on your phone
  6. The payment auto-records as a goal contribution and an expense
Paste M-Pesa SMS Messages
Copy SMS messages from your M-Pesa messages folder and paste them below. Each SMS on a new line or separated by a blank line. The system auto-detects the transaction type and categorises it.
Supported SMS Formats

SENT Money sent to a person

RECEIVED Money received (not imported as expense)

PAYBILL Paid to paybill (KPLC, Zuku, etc.)

TILL Bought goods at a store

WITHDRAWAL ATM / Agent withdrawal

AIRTIME Airtime purchase

Duplicate M-Pesa codes are automatically skipped. Amounts are matched to your expense categories.
